Neon Pulse – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

English

Neon Pulse does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

  • The game does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • The game does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other sensitive permission.
  • Your progress (current level, best results per level and your language choice) is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or choosing "Reset game" in the main menu deletes it.
  • The game currently contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking code. If this changes in a future version, this policy and the Play Store data safety section will be updated before that version is released.

Children: The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
