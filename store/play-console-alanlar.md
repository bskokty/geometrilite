## 2. Ana mağaza girişi — İngilizce (en-US, varsayılan)

**Uygulama adı** (30 sınır, 10 kullanıldı)
```
Neon Pulse
```

**Kısa açıklama** (80 sınır, 71 kullanıldı)
```
One-tap neon platformer. Jump, dodge and survive endless levels.
```

**Tam açıklama** (4000 sınır)
```
Neon Pulse is a fast, one-tap platformer set in a glowing synthwave world. Your cube runs on its own. You decide when to jump.

HOW IT PLAYS
Tap to jump. Hold to jump again the moment you land. Land on blocks, clear spikes and dodge hazards that come from both the floor and the ceiling. Make a mistake and you restart instantly, so you are back in the action in a heartbeat.

ENDLESS LEVELS
There is no last level. Every level is built from obstacle patterns that have been checked to be beatable. Levels get faster and trickier as you go, and the finish line flows straight into the next level.

NEW ABILITIES
From level 6 you unlock mid-air jumps, and you get more of them as you progress. Use them to climb tall walls and slip past ceiling spikes.

FEATURES
• One-tap controls that work on any phone
• Endless levels with rising speed and difficulty
• Floor and ceiling hazards from level 4
• Mid-air jumps from level 6
• Neon synthwave visuals
• Plays in English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Español, Français, Português, Italiano and Русский
• Your progress is saved on your device. No account or sign-up needed
• Free to play with ads. Watch an optional rewarded ad to continue from your last checkpoint
• The game itself works offline. An internet connection is only needed for ads

Can you reach level 20?
```

## 7. Veri güvenliği formu

| Soru | Yanıt |
|---|---|
| Uygulama gerekli kullanıcı verisi türlerinden herhangi birini topluyor veya paylaşıyor mu? | **Evet** |
| Toplanan tüm veriler aktarım sırasında şifreleniyor mu? | **Evet** |

| Konum → Yaklaşık konum (IP adresinden) | Reklam veya pazarlama, Sahtekârlık önleme/güvenlik/uyumluluk |
| Uygulama etkinliği → Uygulama etkileşimleri | Reklam veya pazarlama, Analiz, Sahtekârlık önleme/güvenlik/uyumluluk |
